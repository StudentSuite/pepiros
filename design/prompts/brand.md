# Brand assets — Editorial Paper

Paste this single block into an image/design-generation tool. Structured as distinct, individually-usable deliverables at production dimensions — a favicon, an OG image, logo lockups, an app icon — not one board to crop apart by hand.

> Design production-ready brand assets for **Pepiros**, a grounded research platform where every AI-surfaced claim stays bound to the exact quoted sentence it came from. Style: Editorial Paper, Are.na meets NYT Reader mode. Torn/deckle-edge warm cream paper texture (`#F5F1E8`-ish, visible fiber grain) as the material for every card. Wordmark: "PEPIROS" in wide-tracked Source Serif 4 small caps, near-black ink (`#2A2A28`-ish), refined editorial serif, no rounding or display-font tricks. Mark: a delicate line-art glyph combining an open book and a quill pen, single dark ink color, simple enough to stay legible at 16px. Palette, use exactly: Stone `#B8B2A4`, Clay `#C4A78A`, Sage `#7D8A73`, Dusk `#6E6AA7`, Rose `#B46A6A`, Ochre `#D4B26A`, Teal `#5F8D86`. Typography: Source Serif 4 for headings/display, Inter for UI/secondary. Tagline: "Every claim, one click from its source."
>
> Generate these as separate, individually usable assets, each at its real production dimensions — not cropped from one board:
> 1. **Favicon** — 512×512px master (downsamples cleanly to 32×32/16×16): the book+quill glyph alone on a small cream paper-textured square, clean square edge (no deckle fray — it won't survive a 16px crop), bold and simple.
> 2. **OG / social share image** — 1200×630px landscape. Left third: wordmark lockup + tagline on the paper texture. Right two-thirds: a muted screenshot-style excerpt of the citation-graph view (dark chrome, paper reading card, graph rail) as a soft floating card with a subtle drop shadow. Must read clearly as a link-preview thumbnail.
> 3. **Primary logo lockup (horizontal)** — wordmark + glyph side by side + tagline beneath, dark ink, transparent background, 2400×800px, for light surfaces.
> 4. **Reversed logo lockup** — same composition, warm paper-white ink, transparent background, 2400×800px, for the dark-chrome UI.
> 5. **App icon** — 1024×1024px, the glyph centered on a cream paper-textured rounded-square tile (standard iOS/Android/PWA icon convention), no text.
> 6. **Color palette reference sheet** — the 7 swatches above as torn-paper tiles, hex values labeled in small-caps serif micro-type, for the design-system docs.
>
> Material and lighting throughout: matte paper photography, natural soft light, visible fiber grain on every card, never glossy, never a gradient background, no hard drop shadow anywhere except the one subtle screenshot-card shadow in #2.

**Note:** this produces reference imagery from an image-generation tool. Converting the output into installable `favicon.ico`/SVG/PNG production files is a follow-up export pass.
