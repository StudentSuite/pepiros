# Landing / marketing: Editorial Paper

Grounding-first hero, platform vision below the fold (never the headline). See `docs/PLAN-V1.md §22`.

> A desktop browser mockup of the Pepiros marketing homepage, Editorial Paper direction (Are.na meets NYT Reader mode). Hero: warm paper-textured panel, large serif headline about verifying AI claims against real quoted sources ("every claim, one click from the sentence it came from"), a muted-color node-graph illustration fragment beside it. Below the fold, a calmer, smaller second section introduces the platform vision (upload, discover, discuss) in quieter type, visually secondary to the hero. Dark warm-charcoal chrome, generous whitespace, small-caps serif section labels, calm unhurried layout, soft ambient lighting, 16:9.
